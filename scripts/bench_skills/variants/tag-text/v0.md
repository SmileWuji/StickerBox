# The text on the image

Copy every piece of text on the image, verbatim, into `--text`. People search stickers by the
phrase on them more than by anything else, so this field matters most.

## Answer

Decide first, then commit with the result:

```json
{"has_text": true, "text": "今天也要 摸鱼"}
{"has_text": false, "text": ""}
```

`--text "今天也要 摸鱼"`, or `--text ""` when there is none. The field is required even when
empty, so that "no text" is a decision, not an omission.

## Rules

- Exactly what is written, in its original language and script. Do not translate, correct,
  complete or summarise. Keep `……`, `！？`, emoji drawn as text, and repeated characters.
- Punctuation as drawn. When hand lettering makes width unclear, use full-width marks in Chinese
  and Japanese text (`！`, `？`, `～`, `……`) and half-width in Latin text.
- Line breaks become one space; add no other spaces (none after a comma unless one is drawn).
- All text, in reading order (top to bottom, left to right; right to left for vertical
  Japanese), separated by single spaces.
- Watermarks and handles (`@somechannel`) are text: include them. They are attribution.
- Not text: decorative strokes, speed lines, sound-effect shapes too stylised to read, letters
  on a shirt pattern you cannot read. When unsure whether marks are writing, leave them out.
- For an animation, read the contact sheet from `sticker-box frames`; include text that
  appears in any frame, once.

## The OCR hint

`stage` reports `ocr_hint`: what a small OCR engine found. It is often wrong (wrong characters,
missing lines, text invented from patterns) and it never sees stylised lettering. Use it only to
double-check what you read yourself; never copy it without looking. `noise: true` means the hint
looked like stray marks (a lone letter, digits, a short Latin fragment) rather than words; it
says nothing about whether the image really has text.

## Examples

- A cat with a caption bar reading "Monday again." → `{"has_text": true, "text": "Monday again."}`
- A character with a speech bubble "好耶！" and a small "@artist_handle" in the corner →
  `{"has_text": true, "text": "好耶！ @artist_handle"}`
- A face with exclamation marks drawn beside it and nothing written →
  `{"has_text": false, "text": ""}`
- OCR hint says "0K" on a sticker whose bubble clearly reads "OK!" → `"OK!"`
